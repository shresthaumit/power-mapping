Decolonizing Global Health

Power mapping in global health

Purpose

Global health involves many actors who do not necessarily have equal influence over priorities, resources, knowledge, or decisions. In this activity, you will examine where power sits, how it moves between actors, and what it might take to redistribute it.

Your task

1Position each actor according to their level of power: High, Moderate, or Limited.
2Connect actors with arrows to show how power flows between them.
3Label those relationships using the power labels provided in your scenario.
4Analyze the relationship your group believes creates the greatest power imbalance.
5Redesign one relationship to create a more equitable distribution of power.
The key question: who has the power to shape what happens — and why?
