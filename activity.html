Step 1–3 · Position, connect, and label

Drag each actor into a power zone based on evidence from the scenario. Drag a card back to the tray to move it. Then click two placed actors in a row to draw an arrow between them, and choose which kind of power that relationship represents.

Arrows show the direction of power or influence. You do not need to connect every actor. Only create relationships you can justify from the scenario — and think carefully about which label fits best, since more than one type of power may exist between the same two actors. Click an arrow to remove it.

Step 4 · Analyze

Select the one relationship your group believes creates the greatest power imbalance. Selecting it will highlight that arrow on your map above.
